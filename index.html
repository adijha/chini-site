Chini

Glucose meter sync and logbook for iPhone.

Syncing your meter

  1. Open Chini and tap Add meter.
  2. Press OK on your OneTouch Verio Flex so its screen is on. If no Bluetooth symbol shows, hold OK and the up arrow together to switch Bluetooth on.
  3. Tap the meter when it appears in the list. If your iPhone asks for a code, enter the one shown on the meter.

The first sync reads everything stored on the meter and can take a couple of minutes. Later syncs only fetch new readings.

Common questions

My meter does not appear. It will not show in the iPhone's Bluetooth settings, only inside Chini. Keep the meter's screen on while Chini is looking for it. If the meter is paired with another phone, turn Bluetooth off on that phone.

A reading is in the wrong column. Chini sorts readings into before or after a meal using the meal times in Settings. Adjust them to match when you usually eat.

Which meters work? The OneTouch Verio Flex. More meters are planned. You can also try the app with sample data, without a meter.

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. See the privacy policy.
