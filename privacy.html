Chini Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

Chini does not collect any data. Everything you put into the app, or sync into it, stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • Glucose readings copied from your meter, with their dates and times.
  • The meal times, medicines, doses and target range you enter.
  • A technical log of the Bluetooth connection to your meter, used to diagnose sync problems. It includes the readings as the meter sent them.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Chini has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data.

Bluetooth

Chini uses Bluetooth only to find your glucose meter and read the results stored on it.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Chini creates an image of your table and hands it to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. What happens to it after that is covered by that app's own privacy policy. Chini shares nothing unless you tap Share.

Backups and deleting your data

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Chini's data is included in that backup like any other app's. Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone. Removing a meter inside the app deletes that meter's readings.

Children

Chini does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
